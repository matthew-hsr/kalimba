Privacy

Kalimba (tuner and songs) · last updated 2 November 2026

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Microphone. The tuner uses the microphone only while the tuner is switched on, to measure the pitch of the note you play. The sound is analysed on your device as it happens. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Settings. Your choices (kalimba size, key, how notes are shown) are saved on your device only.

Internet. The app works fully offline and does not send data to any server.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/matthew-hsr/kalimba.
